# Stylized Terrain and World Dressing Reset

The scanned PBR terrain pipeline, runtime texture-array builder, biome material definitions, and tracked scanned texture library were removed. `PlanetTerrainRenderer` now has one active terrain material: a five-color continuous palette with macro variation, slope tint, and wetland darkening. Geometry, displacement, normals, water, world fingerprints, and picking are unchanged.

The production dressing catalog contains eight reviewed Meshy assets under `assets/environment/stylized`: three trees, one bush, one ground-cover form, deadwood, a boulder, and a rock formation. Each maps its supplied LOD0/LOD1/LOD2 directly to tactical/regional/strategic zoom. Tiny cover hides earlier; planet zoom hides everything. The old Poly Haven definitions and primitive fallbacks have no runtime references.

Placement starts with deterministic, continuous sphere-space cluster centers. Five rules compose forest groves, open grassland copses, rough rock fields, low wetland growth, and spaced dryland scrub. Member offsets are continuous world-space tangent offsets. Every accepted member resamples biome weights, climate, fertility, roughness, wetland, slope, water zones, displaced surface position, and surface normal. Unit and headquarters directions exclude nearby presentation props without changing occupancy or gameplay.

Materials are forced non-metallic with high restrained roughness, reduced normal strength, and subtle common-palette tints. Imported meshes are centered laterally and grounded from their transformed bounds. Trees, boulders, and formations cast shadows; smaller vegetation and debris do not. No placeholder geometry remains active; reeds remain a future authored category and are currently omitted.

On Radeon RX 590, Small generated 286 instances from 362 cluster candidates, with seven regional batches visible, 6.935 ms median and 7.306 ms p95 frame time. Medium generated 1,166 instances from 1,448 candidates, with seven regional batches visible, 6.942 ms median and 7.300 ms p95. Both retain 24 total asset/LOD batches and use only one LOD batch per asset at a time. First-process authored GLB loading took 11.49 seconds on Small and 12.55 seconds on Medium; cached rebuilds took 0.23 and 1.13 seconds.

Visual QA captured Forest and Grassland at tactical/regional distance, Rocky, Wetland, and Dryland tactically, a mixed transition, strategic and planet views, plus an occupied-cell overlay view. Focused tests cover shader activation, absence of terrain texture arrays, all 24 LOD mappings, authored-only batches, clustered determinism, cell-center independence, water and slope exclusion, terrain contact, radial alignment, occupied clearings, unchanged gameplay/world state, and displaced picking.
